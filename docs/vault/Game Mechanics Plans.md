**Design Rules**:
- All building blocks **must be renewable** and not a pain to get. Farms are acceptable so long as they don't inevitably break performance when scaled up.

Each track is a game play loop which yields resources for horizontal progression.

## Farming Track
Crop farms with different farm design and crop collection requirements. Creates a renewable resource, that is used and consumed for other game play reasons. Farm requirements bring creativity for their design and decoration.

## Building Track
Establish a base and begin destroying, building and terra forming the land. Building serves two purposes: home personalisation / aesthetics / creativity and game progression via requirements and puzzles. The latter encourages the former via interesting block colours, required compound block structure design which allows and possibly encourages  interesting shapes, sizes and designs.

Purposes:
- Personalising a base and building creatively is enjoyable for most people, most of all artistic types.
- Required compound buildings and puzzle-like building requirements give the aesthetically nice areas a reason to exist, otherwise they would be empty and built in vain.
## Underground Resource Gathering Track (Safe)
Mining expeditions. Easy resource gathering for specific materials that happen to exist in the settled region, potentially gathered for trade.
	- The ability to construct a mine or gather raw resources without going on an expedition makes it safe and easy to gather the pseudo-finite resources found in that area.
		- Ores should regenerate after a long time has passed, much longer than in pre generated structures.
	- Stone mining should be very slow without upgrades, encouraging different play styles. Inside your fortress, there will be speed buffs to mining stone for construction. This can be leveraged to create a mine that leads to useful underground areas.
	- Resources become renewable by adapting the magma layer or volcanoes to produce resources. The resources accumulated from them are of low quality and cannot be used in crafting anything but building blocks / things for aesthetics.

Purposes:
- Expands the building track by enabling the construction and decoration of mines
- Establishes an easy way of gathering building resources.

## Underground Resource Gathering Track (Exploratory)
Leaving your home to collect materials, preparing for the expedition and reaping rewards in needed materials and rare resources. You go explore pre-generated structures underground, create your own mine or reach an area which provides underground resources for fortress construction.
- Outside your own territory or fortress, it should be very unrealistic to establish a mine. The method by which resources are gathered should then be by venturing and exploring pre-generated mines and underground structures.

## Quest Track
An exception to the rule, quests allow for minor vertical progression as a special reward.
