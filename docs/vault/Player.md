Skill Trees: Things you can do in the world that increase your master in a skill tree, enhancing that action. Building up your mastery should not be the exact same as using your mastery in a quest.

For example, potion making mastery would require tools, a workshop, crafting different kinds of potions. An actual potion making quest would require making something esoteric, something with special ingredients or a large quantity of potions which would only be realistically possible to be made via simple automation. The quest is an application of the actions in the skill tree mastery.

Player progression has to be as flat as possible.  This is to ensure replay ability of the world with new quests and cooperative play regardless of total play time in the world. Ways to accomplish this:

- As you gain mastery in one skill tree, you lose mastery in others.
- All natural resources are renewable, all items have durability. Special, strong quest items cannot be repaired, normal base game items can all be repaired forever.
	- The intent here being to strike a balance between rewarding a player for completing a quest with a lasting reward vs. not letting the reward last forever, breaking other quests. You have to choose whether to use up the item to make a quest easier or to frame it as a trophy or to use it only when absolutely neccessary.
	- By limiting the amount of items a player can carry, we can ensure that the player doesn't just bring every possible item for every occasion. It should not be advisable or possible to bring a strong quest item from the past along with your standard loadout for a quest, you have to choose one or the other.
- We limit what the player can do based on their mastery. Building mastery will increase inventory size, resource gathering efficiency, etc. Combat improves stats, but does not increase inventory. In this way we can limit what a player has on them and is able to bring with them or do without explicitly banning items for quests.
	- For example, a quest might require a high mastery in combat. Because the player has this mastery, we know for sure he cannot have multiple sets of armour, weapons etc. which would make the challenge too easy.
	- An issue occurs when other players work together to overcome these restrictions in a way that breaks the game. Having a balanced party is good but steps need to be put in place such that nerfs based around carrying capacity are not broken by builders who require a lot of inventory space for building.
		- Maybe make it possible to teleport items back to base? That way resource gathering operations outside the base don't need extra space. We could then increase inventory space on entering the base.
