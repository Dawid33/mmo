**Goal:** Every land and sea cell of a forged world carries a biome that sits where Earth would put it and has a rule behind it that can be checked: vegetation from plant limits on the monthly climate (coldest month, growing degree days, aridity, dry-season length, a treeline by growing-season temperature), shores from the coast classes, wetlands from the water table, desert surfaces from where sand collects, a patchy fire state between savanna and forest — the 72 biomes of the research as four layers.

**Architecture:** One classifier, `worldgen::forge::biomes`, run ONCE in the forge as a PURE FUNCTION OF THE STORED ARTIFACT (`classify_artifact(&WorldArtifact, &Switches) -> BiomeLayers`): it reads the fields Phases 1b–4 stored (the monthly family, the fine rain raster, heights, the water table, the ice, the province and rock columns, the plate record, the water record's coast and ice planes, lakes, reaches) and calls Phase 3's forge-side functions (`derived::derive`, `koppen::classify`) per fine cell. Eight passes in a fixed order. It fills four stored layers: the `Biome` byte (59 reachable cell biomes: 19 of today's 20 plus 40 appended), a second choice and a weight per cell, an underground flag byte per cell, and point features appended to Phase 4's feature list. **The stored bytes are authoritative**: the runtime never classifies; it reads the biome at the existing dithered point and, in an ecotone, picks the second choice in 8 m patches by an integer hash against the stored weight — exact arithmetic, the same on native and wasm32. `game::classify` and `game::CellClimate` are deleted. The land-use layer is NOT in the artifact (history runs after the forge and changes while the world runs): it is a pure function of the world map and the history's sites, in `game`, shown in the map's tile text (Ruling 10). Terrain, climate, rivers and the sea do not move: the quality gate passes untouched through the whole phase.

**Tech Stack:** Rust; crates `game` (`biome.rs`, `history/rules.rs`, `world_map.rs`, a new `history/land_use.rs`), `worldgen` (the forge, `forged.rs`, `forged_tiles.rs`, `material.rs`, `lib.rs`, `trees/`, `tile_map.rs`, the `forge_proto` and `world_map` examples), `client` (one tile-text call site). No new dependencies. No new block, model or texture.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-earth-like-world-overhaul-design.md` (Intent; Goal 3; Non-goals: ground plants, creatures; Anti-goals: "a biome is on the map with no rule behind it that can be checked against Earth's shares"; Constraints: discriminants append only; Decisions 10–13; the Phases table, row 5; "Left to the builder"). ## Global Constraints

- Do not create git worktrees; work on `develop`. No stash, reset, amend, checkout of files, clean, push. Other sessions stage in the same index: each commit carries only the task's own hunks (`common.md`); the committed-tree compile check is the CONTROLLER's.
- **Tests.** Only regression tests of bugs actually seen and the existing gates are committed. Every "temporary test" below is written first, seen failing, quoted in the implementer's report (name, command, failing then passing output) and DELETED before `just smoke`; a commit adds no `#[test]`. An "evidence command" is a tool run whose output is quoted. **No new gate; NO gate is re-recorded in this phase** — not `forge_quality_matches_baseline`, not a `wasm_determinism` pin, not a physics golden.
- **The order inside every Verify step is fixed:** (1) the temporary tests pass; (2) DELETE them; (3) `just smoke` (cold builds: `just smoke_timeout=1200 smoke`; if its audit fails on a file that is NOT the task's own, run `just test-audit <the task's crate dirs>` plus smoke's remaining steps by hand and say so); (4) `just test-one worldgen "--no-run --tests --examples"` when examples or tests changed or a public `worldgen` symbol changed; (5) `just web-check` when `worldgen` or `game` library code changed; (6) `just determinism-wasm` when `game` changed — in this checkout its `scene_hash` stage differs from its pin because of the owner's uncommitted `crates/game` work: the printed value must be the SAME before and after the task (record both), the recipe stops there, so its remaining stages are run by hand, and **`HISTORY_DIGEST` must not move** (a moved pin is BLOCKED, never a re-record).
- **The safety net of every task** (`L` = the ledger directory, `N` = the task): (1) the gate passes UNTOUCHED: `just test-one worldgen "--test it -- --include-ignored forge_lib::"`; (2) `just forge-report --size dev --cell 32 --seeds 8 > L/tN-32.txt` and `--cell 64 --seeds 8 > L/tN-64.txt` reproduce every line of the previous task's files except the lines the task lists as its own: `diff` prints no `<` line outside that list. **Never allowed to move in any task:** `height_crc`, `artifact_terrain_crc` (Task 1 adds it: every stored field but the biome layers and the regions), `artifact_climate_crc`, `artifact_planet_crc`, `artifact_province_crc`, `artifact_header_crc` (this phase adds no knob), and every line of Phases 1a–4 whose subject is terrain, climate, water or ice. **Biome-derived lines** — the only ones a task may list: keys containing `biome`, `region`, `zonal.`, `fire.`, `desert.`, `shore.`, `underground.`, `points.`, `trees_across_seam`, the score lines, `world_file_bytes`, `artifact_rasters_crc` (its field list holds `biome`, `region`, `regions`) and, from Task 11 (the points join the stored feature list there), the feature counts and the water crc. A moved line outside both lists is BLOCKED.
- **Two regimes.** *Tasks 1–9 change no world*: nothing moves but appended lines (Task 3's none at all). *Task 10 changes stored bytes without changing a biome; Tasks 11–13 change biomes, regions, the runtime's reading of them and the map, on purpose; Tasks 14–15 change no world.* On every run these still hold: the seam rule (`seam_pass_*: yes` for height, precip, biome and the monthly fields), `polar_rows_all_sea: yes`, `aliased_regions_identical: yes`, `world_map_wraps: yes`, `trees_across_seam_ok: yes`.
- **Stored formats.** ONE bump of each, in Task 10: `ARTIFACT_VERSION` 12 → 13, `TILED_FORMAT_VERSION` 5 → 6. `WORLD_MAP_VERSION` 4 → 5 in Task 13. `META_FORMAT` does not move. No other task changes a stored or wire byte layout. Every world is re-forged, never migrated; an older world is refused on every path Phase 1b's Task 3 lists (file, menu, store, `worldctl retile`).
- **`Biome` discriminants are stored bytes: append only, never renumber, never remove.** `game::TileSummary` gains NO field (its bytes are in the pinned history digest): per-tile additions go in parallel vectors of `game::WorldMap`.
- **The classifier is forge-side** (std float math, `powf`, parallel rows through `par::map_range`, independent of the thread count) and a pure function of the artifact. **The runtime's new reads are exact arithmetic only** (add, subtract, multiply, divide, floor, integer hash, the existing dither noise): three byte reads at the existing dithered point and one 64-bit integer mix. - Forge cost is measured, not capped; the design budget is in "Cost".

  | rock classes | — | `acid` = granite, sandstone; `base_rich` = limestone, basalt; `soft` = shale; `soluble` = limestone; `porous` = sandstone, conglomerate, limestone, basalt — the phase's "soil fields" (Ruling 5) |
  | `lake` | `Option<LakeKind>` | the lakes' cell runs |
  | planet reads | — | at `PlanetRecord::cell_at(fx, fz)`: `crust`, `orogeny_kind`, `orogeny_age_my`; `elevation_m` bilinear; the coast record's `class`, `exposure` (÷ 255), `tide_m` (`tide_dm / 10`), `sediment`, `winter_sea_c` |

- **Per-world planes** (`inputs::Planes`, one value per fine cell, built once by linear passes, dropped when the classifier returns): `shore_dist: Vec<u16>` (quarter-cells to the nearest cell of the other kind — land to sea, sea to land — by the forge's existing chamfer `habitat::coast_distance_by`, saturating); `enclosure: Vec<u8>` (share of land cells in the square window of half-width `LAGOON_WINDOW_M = 160` m, × 255, by two running sums); `reach: Vec<u8>` (0 = none; else 1 + the regime of the nearest WET reach whose `REACH_FLOOD_COEFF (20) · sqrt(max monthly flow)` metres reach the cell: a chamfer seeded from the reaches' points) — `flood_reach` = `reach ≠ 0` and that reach's largest monthly flow ≥ `FLOODPLAIN_MIN_FLOW_M3S = 5`; `zonal: Vec<u8>`; `ai_q: Vec<u8>` (`min(255, round(ai · 100))`), `map_q: Vec<u8>` (`min(255, map / 16)`); `flags: Vec<u8>`. Eight bytes per fine cell. **On the climate grid** (≤ 784 × 392): the sand sink score (Ruling 6).
- **`BiomeLayers`** `{ biome: Vec<u8>, biome2: Vec<u8>, weight: Vec<u8>, underground: Vec<u8>, points: Vec<Feature> }`.
- **Second choice and weight.** `biome2[i]` = the cell's other biome; `weight[i]` ∈ 0 ..= 127 = the second choice's share of the cell's mosaic in 1 / 255 (127 ≈ half; the primary is by definition the larger). Zonal cells (pass 4): every threshold test goes through `zonal::Probe` — `above(value, threshold, width) -> bool` records the test's normalised margin `m = (value − threshold) / width`; the cell's second choice is the result of the SAME tree run once more with the one test of smallest `|m|` flipped, and `weight = round(127 · max(0, 1 − |m|))`; if that run gives the same biome, `biome2 = biome`, `weight = 0`. Widths: `BLEND_T_C = 1.0` (every temperature threshold), `BLEND_GDD = 0.1 ·` threshold, `BLEND_AI = 0.1 ·` threshold, `BLEND_DRY = 0.5` month, `BLEND_MM = 0.1 ·` threshold, `BLEND_SEASON_DAYS = 10`. Cells an override took (passes 2, 3, 5): `biome2` = the ZONAL biome underneath (the potential natural vegetation), `weight = 0` except a wetland's and an oasis's fringe (`weight = 127` at the byte limit itself, 64 one step inside, else 0). Sea cells and fire-band cells: `biome2 = biome`, `weight = 0` (the fire edge is sharp on purpose).
- **The runtime mosaic** (`ForgedWorld::biome_at`, Task 12): `(dx, dz) = dithered(x, z)` as today; `i = nearest_cell(dx, dz)`; `(b1, b2, w)` = the three stored bytes; if `w == 0` → `b1`. Else, with `(ax, az)` = the dithered point in map metres (x folded into `[0, width)` on a world that wraps) and `MOSAIC_PATCH_M = 8.0`: `px = floor(ax / 8) as i64`, `pz = floor(az / 8) as i64`, `roll = (mix64(seed ^ MOSAIC_SALT, px as u64, pz as u64) >> 56) as u8` (`mix64` = the splitmix-style mix `trees::placement::hash64` uses, as a function of `forged.rs`); `roll < w` → `b2`, else `b1`. Two neighbouring cells of an ecotone hold each other as second choice with complementary weights, so the patches cross the cell border without a step.
- **Underground flags** (`u8`): bits 0–1 aquifer (`0` none, `1` light, `2` heavy), bit 2 dry caves, bit 3 underground water, bit 4 sea caves. `UNDERGROUND_AQUIFER_MASK = 0b11`, `UNDERGROUND_CAVES = 4`, `UNDERGROUND_WATER = 8`, `UNDERGROUND_SEA_CAVES = 16`; a byte above 31 is invalid.
- **Desert cells** (the denominator of the sand share) = land cells whose biome is `SandDesert`, `RockyWasteland`, `Badlands` with `ai < AI_ARID`, `ColdDesert`, `HyperAridDesert` or `SaltFlat`. **Forest biomes** (the treeline line, `lib.rs` `forest`, the forest share) = `Taiga`, `ConiferForest`, `BroadleafForest`, `MixedForest`, `TemperateRainforest`, `LaurelForest`, `MediterraneanWoodland`, `TropicalConiferForest`, `TropicalBroadleafForest`, `TropicalSeasonalForest`, `TropicalDryForest`, `CloudForest`, `ThornWoodland`, `Swamp`, `MangroveSwamp`, `PeatSwampForest`. **Wetlands** = `Marsh`, `Swamp`, `MangroveSwamp`, `SaltMarsh`, `Mudflat`, `PeatBog`, `Fen`, `PeatSwampForest`, `FloodedGrassland`, `Floodplain`. **Water biomes** (`report::is_water`, capacity 0) = `Ocean`, `FrozenOcean`, `Lake`, `ShelfSea`, `AbyssalPlain`, `Trench`, `Lagoon`, `CoralReef`, `KelpForest`, `SeagrassMeadow`, `SaltLake`.
- **Switches** (`biomes::Switches`, all `true` in every caller but the tool; `ForgeParams::features` gains `pub biomes: biomes::Switches`; names of `--off` in the forge-feature set, added by the task that reads each): `treeline` (off: the treeline test always passes), `mangrove_limits` (off: the two temperature tests and the exposure test pass), `sand_sinks` (off: every flat arid cell is sand, no cap), `fire` (off: the band keeps its zonal biomes), `shore`, `wetlands`, `mosaic` (off: every weight 0).
- **Report lines** of this phase are appended at the END of `report::report`'s rows, in task order. `biome_share.<Name>` keeps its meaning (share of LAND cells; one row per land variant of `Biome::ALL`, 0 when absent — `Mountain` included); new: `sea_biome_share.<Name>` (share of sea cells, per sea variant).

## Rulings (made while writing this plan; each `what — why — cost if wrong`, with the alternative rejected)

1. **The 72 are assigned as the table has them: 59 cell biomes, 5 objects and points, 4 underground flags, 4 land uses; `Mountain` is retired in place.** The cell layer takes every entry whose extent is an area a 16–32 m cell can carry and whose cover, trees or map colour differ; sea depth classes (`ShelfSea`, `Ocean`, `AbyssalPlain`, `Trench`) are cell biomes read from the plate record's bathymetry, because the design wants them as "labels on the map" and the map's label IS the tile's biome — rejected: keeping the cell layer at fifty by folding (laurel into broadleaf, the three tropical forests into one, kelp and seagrass into shelf sea): each fold deletes a rule the research gives separately, and a variant costs one row per table — cost if wrong: variants that never appear (the closing task's presence line names them; an absent one is a ruling, not a removal — discriminants cannot be removed).
2. **The classifier runs in the forge only, as a pure function of the stored artifact; the stored bytes are authoritative; the runtime never classifies.** That is what the tree does today (`regions::derive`, `biome_cell`) and what the new passes need: the shore strip, the wetland ladder, oases, the sand sinks and the fire state read NEIGHBOURS and a whole-world quantile, and the plant limits need Thornthwaite's power — none of it can be a per-column exact-arithmetic function. Reading ONLY the artifact (not `Forged`) keeps `derive`'s property "the raster is exactly the classifier of what a reader sees", lets `WorldArtifact::with_regions` classify a hand-built world, and makes a tuning round a re-classification of saved world files (seconds a world: `forge_proto --biomes --worlds …`, Task 1) instead of a forge — rejected: classifying from `Forged` (it could read Phase 4's stamp kinds and the float climate, and would be unusable on a stored world); a runtime `classify` per column (today's `game::classify` is deleted: nothing calls it at runtime) — cost if wrong: the shore strip re-derived from coast classes and heights disagrees with what Phase 4 stamped; `shore.stamp_agreement` (the share of Phase 4's `Beach` and `TidalFlat` stamp cells that got a shore biome, computed in the report where `Forged` exists) must be ≥ 0.9 or Task 9 moves the strip's limits.
3. **Borders: the stored second choice and weight, drawn by a hashed 8 m mosaic at the dithered point.** Today's 24 m displacement stays (it bends the cell edge); the mosaic adds the ecotone the research asks for, with a weight that comes from how close the deciding threshold is — rejected: perturbing the climate inputs with noise before classifying (the note's first choice: it moves the forge's biome away from the stored climate, so the Köppen cross-check and the treeline line would be judged on inputs nobody can read back); blending covers at runtime by bilinear weights (needs four cells' pairs per column and has no meaning for a tree list) — cost if wrong: salt-and-pepper at 8 m; `MOSAIC_PATCH_M` and the blend widths are Task 9's dials, and `--off mosaic` is the fallback (weight 0 everywhere).
4. **Moisture is the aridity index `MAP / Σ PET`, not Prentice's α.** UNEP's classes were read, the program's dryland evidence is defined on it, IUCN's desert profiles state the same ratios, and it needs no soil bucket size; BIOME1's α thresholds could not be read at all (the linked file is another paper). `Derived::alpha` is not read by any rule — cost if wrong: Thornthwaite's evapotranspiration is capped at 26.5 °C and reads low in hot deserts (Phase 3's Known limit), so the index reads high there and drylands come out small: the dryland line shows it, and the remedy is Phase 3's dial, not a threshold here (Task 9 may move `AI_SEMI_DESERT` only).
5. **"Soil fields" are five rock classes and the slope, computed per cell and not stored.** Depth, texture and fertility rasters have no reader in this phase (no farming, no ground plants: Non-goals); what the classifier needs of soil is whether the rock is acid or base-rich (bog against fen, heath), soft (badlands), soluble and porous (the underground flags), and how steep the ground is (bare rock, shrubland, sand). The design's Phases row is amended in Task 16 — rejected: a stored soil byte "for later" (a format nobody reads cannot be judged) — cost if wrong: a farming phase adds its own rasters in its own bump.
6. **Sand is where it collects, and at most 30 % of desert by construction.** On the climate grid, for cells with `ai < AI_ARID`: `sink = SAND_W_CONV · c + SAND_W_BASIN · b`, `c` = the convergence of the year's mean wind (minus the divergence of the stored wind planes' annual mean, by central differences, x wrapping, clamped at 0 and scaled by `SAND_CONV_FULL`), `b` = how far the cell's mean ground lies below the mean of the ring `SAND_BASIN_RING_DEG = 3°` around it (clamped at 0, scaled by `SAND_BASIN_FULL_M = 50`); bilinear to the fine grid. A flat arid fine cell is sand when its score ≥ `max(SAND_SINK_MIN, q)`, `q` = the score's quantile such that sand is `SAND_DESERT_SHARE_MAX` of the world's desert cells (one histogram pass); then one 3 × 3 majority pass on the sand mask. The report prints the capped and the uncapped share — rejected: sand = flat arid ground (today's "waterlogged desert": on a flat pocket desert it is most of it); a sediment-transport simulation (the note's "sand supply" field: a forge stage of its own for one biome) — cost if wrong: the cap, not the rule, sets the share on every seed (`desert.sand_capped_seeds` counts them); then the weights are Task 9's.
7. **The fire state is a thresholded smooth field with hysteresis, stored as the biome itself.** In the band (tropical ∧ `FIRE_BAND_MM` ∧ `dry < FIRE_BAND_MAX_DRY_MONTHS`): `u` = a two-octave value noise of wavelength `FIRE_PATCH_M` in true metres (periodic in x), mapped to 0 … 1; `p` = `FIRE_P_FOREST` at the cell's rainfall, + `FIRE_REFUGE_BONUS 0.2` where slope > 0.2 or `reach ≠ 0` (gallery forest), − `FIRE_SAND_PENALTY 0.2` on sandstone; a cell is a forest SEED when `u < p − FIRE_HYSTERESIS`, a CANDIDATE when `u < p + FIRE_HYSTERESIS`; forest = seeds and every candidate 8-connected to a seed through candidates (one breadth-first pass, x wrapping: order-independent). Forest cells take the forest type their dry season gives, the others `Savanna`. "Persistent" means stored: there is no fire raster and nothing burns at runtime — rejected: a per-patch hash (square patches); cellular-automaton smoothing passes (their result depends on the pass count, not on a rule) — cost if wrong: the boundary still follows the rainfall contour; `fire.edge_rain_spread_mm` (the standard deviation of rainfall along forest–savanna edges inside the band) must exceed the same line with `--off fire`.
8. **Köppen is computed, never stored per cell.** It is a function of the stored normals with compares and sums only; the report, `world-map --layer koppen` (Phase 3) and the world map's builder compute it where they need it. The game shows it as tile text: `WorldMap::koppen` holds three ASCII bytes per tile (the class of the tile's most common land cell class) — rejected: a Köppen byte raster (78.7 MB raw at Large / 16 m for a value three tools can recompute) — cost if wrong: none (a raster can be added in a later bump).
9. **The underground layer is stored, one byte per cell, though nothing generates from it yet.** Its rule reads the annual runoff (Thornthwaite), so a later cave or aquifer generator could not compute it at runtime; storing it in this phase's one bump spares that work a re-forge of every world. Its only readers now are the report, a map layer and the tile text, and the plan says so — rejected: computing it on demand in tools only (then "the 72 as four layers" has a layer no stored world carries) — cost if wrong: a byte per cell whose values a real cave generator redefines (the flags are append-only bits; bits 5–7 are free).
10. **The land-use overlay is not in the artifact and not in generated terrain: it is a pure function of the world map and the history's sites.** History runs AFTER the forge (`bootstrap_history` on the server / coordinator, from the world map) and its sites are founded and abandoned while the world runs; `generate_region` is a pure function of coordinates plus the stored FORGED world and cannot see it; a stored land-use raster would be a second copy of history that two processes could disagree on. So: `game::LandUse` and `game::history::land_use::overlay(map, sites)` (integer arithmetic, deterministic), computed by whoever shows it — in this phase the client's map window, from the `WorldMap` and the `SiteMarker`s it already holds, as one line of tile text. The natural biome is never overwritten ("stored OVER the natural biome" holds because the overlay's source — the history — is the stored thing). No field, pasture or plantation is generated as blocks (no art in this program); settlements are whatever the site code builds — rejected: painting land use into the biome raster at forge time (there is no history yet); a per-region raster in the world store (a table the coordinator and the hosts would both need to write) — cost if wrong: the owner wants fields in the terrain: that is a content phase reading this same function. **For the controller: this is a reduced first form of the program's "a land-use overlay from history over the natural biome"; it is stated, not hidden.**
11. **`RegionType::Mountains` is the alpine belt** (`AlpineTundra`, `TropicalAlpine`, `BareRock`, and the retired `Mountain`); nothing reads a height. Small worlds have mountains of a few hundred metres (Phase 2's Ruling 3): there the belt exists only where latitude already brings the treeline near the ground, and the score's "mountain starts" reads low on Dev and Tiny as it does after Phase 2 — rejected: a relief or height threshold for the region type (the rule this phase retires) — cost if wrong: mountain regions near the polar treeline on worlds without mountains; the hand check names it.
12. **Tuning is one task with a cap** (Task 9): at most FOUR rounds, each a re-classification of the 16 saved worlds; after the fourth, every remaining miss is a ruling with its number. No new mechanism inside a tuning round.
13. **The evidence is judged on MEDIUM for presence and position, on both sizes for shares.** Known before it is measured: on Dev and Tiny there is no alpine belt by elevation, so `CloudForest`, `TropicalConiferForest` and `TropicalAlpine` are absent there; **`TropicalAlpine` is expected absent at EVERY size** (the peak cap is 2,800 m: at a 27 °C sea-level equator and the default lapse the ground there is near 9 °C, above the 6.4 °C treeline) and is recorded as a ruling unless a seed shows it; lakes, and so `SaltLake` and `SaltFlat`, are few on small worlds; polar land is scarce at every size (polar seas), so ice, tundra and polar desert shares are printed, not judged.
14. **An evidence line counts only if it fails with its feature off** (Phase 3's rule): each judged line names its switch in the closing table and its off-run value is measured in Task 9's round 0; a line that still passes with its feature off is struck from the evidence there and the task says what replaced it.

## Review Focus

1. **A world that changes before Task 10, or terrain that changes at all.** `regions::derive` must keep calling `game::classify` until Task 11; nothing in this phase may touch a height, a climate value, a river or a lake. The proof is `height_crc` and `artifact_terrain_crc` on 16 seeds, not a reading of the diff.
2. **The datum and the bit-identity.** Land rules read normals AT THE GROUND from the stored container by the runtime's own expression; sea rules read sea level; "trees at sea level" reads sea level on purpose. A classifier fed the forge's float climate, or the annual raster, passes every share check and breaks `biome_inputs_match_runtime`.
3. **Pass order and what an override keeps.** A later pass may only take cells its rule names; every override stores the zonal biome underneath as second choice; the fire pass runs on the zonal result, never on an override. A wetland on a glacier, a beach on a lake shore, a mangrove inland are order bugs.
4. **The seam and the poles in every neighbourhood pass.** The chamfers, the running sums, the reach raster, the fire field's noise and its breadth-first pass, the majority filters, the planet-cell trench search: x by `rem_euclid`, rows clamped. `seam_pass_biome` and `trees_across_seam_ok` are the lines.
5. **Exhaustive consumers.** Sixty variants through `ground_cover`, `region_type`, `biome_capacity_per_mille`, the density table, the colour table: a wildcard arm that silently gives a harsh biome full capacity or temperate cover is the audit's named failure. Existing arms keep their values exactly (`HISTORY_DIGEST`).
6. **Exact arithmetic at runtime.** The mosaic is an integer mix of patch coordinates folded by the world's period; a float hash, a patch index computed in engine metres instead of map metres, or a fold that differs between a whole and a tiled world breaks `aliased_regions_identical` or wasm.
7. **By-construction evidence.** The sand cap, the treeline line and the mangrove line are true by how the rule is written; each must be shown to FAIL with its switch off, and the uncapped sand share is the number that says whether the rule works.
8. **Whole world ≠ tiled world.** Three new byte rasters through `split`, `encode_tile`, `decode_checked` (a biome byte ≥ 60, an underground byte > 31, a weight > 127 are refused); `biome_at` must agree at tile borders.

**The decision tree** (first match; `T` = "tropical", constants by name from the thresholds table):

1. `slope > BARE_ROCK_MIN_SLOPE` → `BareRock`.
2. no `trees` (skipped when `!sw.treeline`): (a) no `trees_sea`: `map < POLAR_DESERT_MAX_MM ∨ gdd0 < VEGETATION_MIN_GDD0` → `PolarDesert`, else `Tundra`; (b) `permanent_snow ∨ gdd0 < VEGETATION_MIN_GDD0` → `BareRock`; (c) `t_range < TROPICAL_ALPINE_MAX_RANGE_C ∨ |lat| < 23.5` → `TropicalAlpine`; (d) `AlpineTundra`.
3. `ai < AI_HYPER_ARID` → `HyperAridDesert`.

6. `ai < AI_SEMI_DESERT` → `ThornScrub` when T, else `SemiDesert`.
7. `ai < AI_DRYLAND`: T → `ThornWoodland` when `ai < AI_SEMI_ARID ∨ dry ≥ 7.5`, else `Savanna`; `summer_dry` → `MediterraneanScrub` when `ai < AI_SEMI_ARID`, else `MediterraneanWoodland`; `ai < AI_SEMI_ARID` → `Shrubland` when `slope ≥ SHRUB_MIN_SLOPE`, else `Grassland`; else `WoodlandSavanna`.
8. Humid: T → `TropicalBroadleafForest` (`dry < 2.5`), `TropicalSeasonalForest` (`< 4.5`), `TropicalDryForest` (`< 7.5`), else `ThornWoodland`; then `t_cold_sea ≥ 18 ∧ ai ≥ CLOUD_FOREST_MIN_AI ∧ ¬winter_dry` → `CloudForest`; `t_cold ≥ 5 ∧ winter_dry ∧ |lat| < 30` → `TropicalConiferForest`; `summer_dry ∧ t_cold > 0` → `MediterraneanWoodland` (`ai < MEDITERRANEAN_MAX_AI`) or `ConiferForest`; `−2 ≤ t_cold < 10 ∧ t_warm < TEMPERATE_RAINFOREST_MAX_WARM_C ∧ map ≥ TEMPERATE_RAINFOREST_MIN_MAP_MM` → `TemperateRainforest`; `t_cold ≥ TEBE_MIN_COLD_C ∧ gdd5 ≥ GDD5_BROADLEAF ∧ map ≥ LAUREL_MIN_MAP_MM` → `LaurelForest`; `t_warm < HEATH_MAX_WARM_C ∧ ai ≥ HEATH_MIN_AI ∧ acid rock` → `Heath`; `gdd5 ≥ GDD5_BROADLEAF` → `BroadleafForest` (`t_cold > BOREAL_MAX_COLD_C`) or `MixedForest`; `gdd5 ≥ GDD5_CONIFER` → `ConiferForest`; else `Heath`.

## Known limits (not in this plan)

- **A biome is a rule, a cover, a tree list, a colour, a capacity** — in EXISTING blocks and the eight existing species. Many biomes share a look: laurel, seasonal and cloud forest wear temperate grass; peat bog and heath differ by a block; an inland oasis grows acacias (the palm's salinity niche keeps it near the sea). New art is follow-up work (Decision 13).
- **The land-use overlay is a function, not terrain** (Ruling 10): no field, pasture or plantation is generated; the map names the use of a tile from the sites the client holds. Nothing is "stored over the natural biome" but the history itself.
- **The underground layer has no generator**: flags per cell say where an aquifer or a cave belongs; nothing digs one. **Point features are map entries**: a spring is not a water source, a vent is a name on the sea.
- **Rivers and deltas are objects, not cell biomes**; the riparian corridor is the `Floodplain` biome and the fire state's refuge bonus, not a gallery-forest variant per forest type.
- **The treeline is thermal only**: Paulsen & Körner's snow-pack and drought terms are not built (a dry lower treeline comes from the aridity cuts); aspect, frost hollows and wind exposure are not modelled (no sub-cell terrain modifiers); the nival belt is `BareRock`, and the runtime's snow still follows the annual-mean snow line until Phase 6.
- **Cloud forest is a temperature band on tropical mountains** (no humidity or cloud-base field exists); fog deserts are not distinguished; there is no Massenerhebung shift.
- **Small worlds**: alpine biomes by latitude only on Dev and Tiny; `TropicalAlpine` is expected on no size at the default peak cap (Ruling 13); mountain REGIONS are the alpine belt (Ruling 11).
- **Sand is capped, not simulated**: no sediment budget, no dune fields as landforms (the cover is sand; the ground is the eroded surface).
- **The fire state is drawn once** in the forge; nothing burns, and the temperate forest–steppe band is zonal (`WoodlandSavanna`), not rolled.
- **Sea biomes are labels and a floor cover**: reefs, kelp and seagrass add no blocks; `AbyssalPlain` and `Trench` lie over a playable floor at −90 m; the trench is a rule beside young arcs (Phase 2 makes no trench depth).
- **Mangroves follow IUCN's air-temperature limits** (coldest month ≥ 20 °C, range ≤ 10 °C): the humid outliers to 38° S that Earth has are excluded.
- **Moisture is the aridity index with Thornthwaite's evapotranspiration** (Ruling 4): drylands inherit its cap in hot deserts. Prentice's α is not used; BIOME1's table was not read.
- **Disturbance patches** (fire scars, windthrow, succession ages), **soil rasters** (depth, texture, fertility: Ruling 5) and **per-species climate envelopes beyond the existing niches** are not built.
- **Köppen is not stored per cell** (Ruling 8): the map holds it per tile.
- **Phase 6 reads**: `ForgedWorld::biome_at` (the mosaic) for what stands on a column; whether a column's trees are in leaf is a species matter, not a biome one, and is Phase 6's.
- **The quality gate still guards land share, hypsometry and rivers only**: every biome claim rests on `forge_proto --report` and `--biomes`.
