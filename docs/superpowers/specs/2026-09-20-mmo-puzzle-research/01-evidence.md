# MMO puzzle research — evidence and sources (2026-09-20)

Condensed from four web-research subagent reports. Flags: **[V]** confirmed in a source
fetched that session; **[S]** search snippet / headline only; **[M]** the agent's memory,
not re-verified; **[?]** weakly sourced. Unflagged lines have a URL the agent fetched.
Where a source refused access it was recorded as a gap, not worked around.

Known disagreements between reports:
- Escape Simulator workshop size: 2,500+ vs 4,000+ rooms (different dates).
- Sky downloads: "100M+" [M] vs 300M+ (pocketgamer.biz) — prefer the sourced figure.
- Uru's Ahnonay: one report says it was retrofitted for *solo* play after the service
  collapsed, another that it was altered to *need* multiple players [?]. Both may be
  true at different times; unverified.

## A. Puzzle content at MMO / shared-world scale

**Uru / Myst Online (Cyan).** Personal instanced hub + per-player instanced Ages you can
invite others into; only the city and neighbourhoods shared. A few Ages needed 2–3
coordinated players; retrofits for solo were bad (a 14-minute real-time wait). Ubisoft
service cancelled 2004, GameTap run 2007–08 cancelled for lack of subscribers; fan-run
MOULa since 2010, engine open-sourced, fan Ages official since 2020. Rand Miller:
"It's not like you can start up a new TV network and give one show a month." GameTap's
Blake Lewin: no budget would have worked "due to the difficult content production
pipeline". Reviewers: "a pretty chat room". Celia Pearce: ~10,000 beta "refugees" formed
durable diaspora communities elsewhere.

**Yohoho! Puzzle Pirates (Three Rings).** Every activity a skill minigame; each crew
member's station puzzle feeds the ship; crafting puzzles generate labour for a
player-run economy; NPC "swabbies" fill empty stations [M]; visible duty ratings let
officers remove lazy crew [M]. Built by 3–6 people; ~25% of the code was puzzles;
4M registered by 2008; ~$230k/month microtransactions + ~$70k/month subscriptions;
still running under Grey Havens. Daniel James (GDC 2005): "Fun is fun, not graphics";
social groups + economy gave the replayability; players needed a mission system;
community "too nice" for PvP.

**The Secret World (Funcom).** Investigation missions: no markers, real-world research
through an in-game browser (Joel Bylos: "solving puzzles through Google"). The browser
opened real Google; result snippets were themselves spoilers. Handmade missions,
cutscenes and VO were cost drivers (Romain Amiel). ~200k sold at launch vs 500k beta
signups; impairment; F2P relaunch 2017. Community view (anecdotal): a few solved,
everyone else used guides.
https://www.tentonhammer.com/articles/creating-the-secret-world-an-interview-with-joel-bylos ·
https://forums.mmorpg.com/discussion/375149/column-the-secret-world-the-ups-and-downs-of-investigation ·
https://www.engadget.com/2012/08/28/the-secret-world-sales-at-200-000-funcom-focused-on-the-future/

**Islands of Insight (Lunarch/Behaviour, Feb 2024).** "Shared-world puzzle game", ~10,000
Witness-style and logic-grid puzzles, many generated and respawning; Metacritic 79.
Per-player puzzle state, no collaboration mechanic ("no way to collaborate on
puzzles"), Steam complaints of lag and "awkward competition over puzzles". Offline mode
added July 2024 and preferred. Peak 7,755 concurrent (during a giveaway). 20+ staff laid
off. Servers closed 30 Oct 2024; converted to offline-only.
https://www.pcgamer.com/games/puzzle/finally-an-online-game-shutting-down-the-right-way-islands-of-insights-will-close-servers-next-month-and-permanently-modify-the-game-to-be-offline-only-so-the-promising-puzzler-can-live-on/ ·

**A Tale in the Desert (eGenesis, Andrew Tepper, 2003–).** No combat. Seven disciplines ×
seven Tests; all-seven makes an Oracle who builds that discipline's Monument; players
"can, as a group, win the game by completing all seven Monuments"; monument builders
design Tests for the next Telling. Tests are mostly social/competitive with top-N
passes: Bureaucrat (a pyramid anyone can seize and wipe), Pathmaker/Venery (players
build puzzles and lockbox hunts; "the 2 highest-rated puzzles each week pass"),
Marriage, Mentorship, Demi-Pharaoh (elected; may ban up to seven accounts). Law system
as constrained Nomic — "players have largely resisted using laws to force behavior".
Developer-run provocations (vandal "Mafia", trader "Malaki", clear-cutting: 3,450 of
15,300 trees cut before laws, a plague). World ends and restarts every ~1.5–2.5 years
(T1 2003 … T10 2021). Tepper: reset "makes for an exciting and fast-paced start-of-Tale,
but a slower endgame". Population 1,000–2,000 (2006); ~150 daily now [?].

**Glitch / Journey / Sky.** Glitch: non-combat browser MMO, "unlaunched" after two
months, closed Dec 2012; assets CC0; the chat tool became Slack. Journey: anonymous
two-player drop-in, one chirp, proximity only helps [M]; Chen removed player collision
after testers pushed each other off ledges [M, GDC 2013]. Sky: doors needing 2/4/8
players [M], hand-holding, chat unlocked by investing in a friendship, gifting instead
of self-purchase (Chen, GDC 2022): "doing this together will leave you better off than
by yourself". 300M+ downloads.

**Hive-mind puzzles in live games.** Destiny 2 *Niobe Labs* (2019): community puzzle gated
a paid forge, unsolved 80+ h, a hint was missing; Bungie unlocked it — "setting this
puzzle up as a gate… has not been an ideal experience". *Corridors of Time* (2020): each
player's instance showed a unique symbol fragment; ~a week of crowdsourced screenshots;
praised because anyone could contribute, criticised because the reward was datamined.
WoW Secret Finding Discord (Kosumoth, Lucid Nightmare, Hivemind — 5 players, ~90
coordinated steps; designer: "We will never provide any hints"; cosmetic rewards).
GW2 jumping puzzles: execution resists spoilers, mesmer portals skip them; disabled in
elite puzzles. RuneScape clue scrolls: randomised step chains, fully wiki-documented,
still farmed for the loot roll. Fez monolith brute-forced; Noita eye glyphs unsolved
5+ years; Curiosity: labour not puzzle, prize never delivered.

**ARGs.** I Love Bees: 100k+ players, payphone GPS puzzle; McGonigal's "stimulating
ambiguity" — puzzles unsolvable by one person, massively distributed data, live
puppet-mastering. Potato Sack (2011): puzzle types spread across player archetypes;
final phase a play-time grind — "Given the toss-up between media attention and player
goodwill, we'll choose the player every time." Audience is a pyramid: few solvers, more
contributors, mostly spectators.
https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/the-portal-two-arg-the-whole-story ·

**Asynchronous help.** Dark Souls/Elden Ring templated, position-anchored, rated
messages — lossy hints. Death Stranding: shared structures, likes-only, decay.

## B. Small-group co-op puzzle adventures

**Portal 2 co-op [V commentary].** Ping tool because telling a partner where to look
"was going to be really hard"; synced 3-2-1 countdown because syncing over voice under
lag failed; gestures unlocked gradually; death made cheap and funny. Chambers bounced
if solvable with one portal pair [S].
https://theportalwiki.com/wiki/Portal_2_developer_commentary

**We Were Here series [V].** Separated players, walkie-talkie only; "librarian" and
"explorer" roles make solo completion structurally impossible. Tiered hints criticised
as generic; disconnect bugs [S]. "25 million players" incl. the free first game [S].
https://premortem.games/2022/05/31/total-mayhem-games-are-masters-of-the-asymmetrical-puzzler/

**Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes [V].** "The fun really lives between players"; tuned
for verbal ambiguity; hidden pacing adds complications when a team is coasting;
randomised bombs defeat memorisation — the one durable replayability answer among
asymmetric games.
https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/finding-the-fun-in-bomb-defusal-with-i-keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes-i-

**The Past Within / Bokura / Operation: Tango / Tick Tock.** Two devices, no shared
screen; near-zero replayability. Rusty Lake: testers are "burned" once they know a
solution, some puzzles changed ~20 times [S]; 1M sold at $6 [V]. Bokura 600k [S].
https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-rusty-lakes-immersive-puzzle

**Hazelight.** Complementary abilities; a new mechanic roughly every 15 minutes then
discarded [V]; Friend's Pass. It Takes Two 30M+, Split Fiction 7M [S].
https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2025/03/05/split-fiction-josef-fares-interview/

**Escape Simulator [V].** 2M+ copies, >75% play co-op, DLC attach >30%, workshop as
content engine. With eight players: "six players standing around watching two others
solve a lock" [S] — puzzle density, not headcount, sets useful group size.
https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-escape-simulator-hit-escape-velocity

**Bodies as tools.** Pico Park creator [V]: "Making one stage is like making seven
stages"; player-to-player collision needed custom physics + rollback netcode; "game
design that generates conversation". Human Fall Flat: 8-player online added late
lifted sales sharply [S]. Trine 4 re-authors puzzles for multiplayer [S].
https://note.com/tecopark/n/n213b54be7d7a?hl=en

**Larger groups.** Among Us tasks (parallel decomposition, count-indifferent) [S]; Deep
Rock Galactic scales enemies per player and keeps class tools helpful-not-mandatory [S];
Animal Well's mural needed ~50 players' fragments, solved on Discord in <24 h [S].
https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Difficulty_Scaling · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Well

## C. Voxel, sandbox and UGC puzzle ecosystems

**Minecraft adventure maps.** Adventure Mode: break only with `CanDestroy` tools, place
only against `CanPlaceOn` blocks; levers/buttons still work — deny by default, whitelist
verbs. CTM (Vechs' Super Hostile) takes the other route: editing stays on and the
challenge is combat, traversal and scarcity. Hypixel's adventure lobby was discontinued
partly because newer versions changed redstone behaviour and broke the maps.
https://minecraft.fandom.com/wiki/Adventure ·
https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/complete-monument ·
https://hypixel.fandom.com/wiki/Adventure_Lobby

**Redstone / Zachtronics.** Redstone: Turing-complete signal propagation; depth and
fragility both come from tick-order quirks. Infinifactory: fixed inputs and required
output, any working factory passes, histograms of cycles/footprint/blocks against all
players — a score does not reveal a solution.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinifactory ·
https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/-i-infinifactory-i-and-the-next-generation-of-the-i-minecraft-i-genre-

**Minecraft MMO servers.** Wynncraft: staff-built non-editable world [?]; raids for a
party of 4 with randomly selected challenge rooms; ~122 wiki pages of Secret
Discoveries; players split between completionists who use the wiki and explorers who
call organic discovery "one of the best parts". Hypixel SkyBlock Catacombs: instanced,
procedurally assembled from authored rooms, ≥2 non-duplicate puzzle rooms per run
(Water Board, Ice Fill, Three Weirdos, Boulder, …); client mods highlight correct
answers; the two-player communication puzzle (Bomb Defuse) was removed. Per-player
fake blocks via `sendBlockChange` [?] — known hazard: server and client blocks disagree.
https://wynncraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Nest_of_the_Grootslangs ·
https://forums.wynncraft.com/threads/have-you-used-the-wiki-to-figure-out-secret-discoveries.315052/ ·
https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeon_Puzzle_Rooms ·
https://bukkit.org/threads/using-packets-to-show-different-blocks-to-different-players.153758/

**Roblox (Doors, Piggy).** Parties of 4 → 6, procedural rooms, light puzzles, threat
pressure rather than difficulty; with strangers, what works is parallelisable
fetch-and-use, legible state without voice, and progress that counts for everyone
[partly agent synthesis].
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doors_(game)

**UGC ecosystems.** Mario Maker 2: difficulty from clear rate, Endless queue + skip as
crowd filter, creator must clear before upload [M]. LittleBigPlanet: x2/x3/x4 areas
reward only prize bubbles, never main-path progress. Dreams: discovery was the main
problem; hand curation ended 2024.
https://supermariomaker2.fandom.com/wiki/Endless_Challenge ·
https://littlebigplanet.fandom.com/wiki/Co-operative_challenge ·
https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2024/03/media-molecules-live-curation-support-for-dreams-stops-next-month

**Systemic design.** BotW (GDC 2017): physics + rule-based "chemistry engine",
"multiplicative" design; shrines accept any route to the goal. Teardown (Gustafsson):
"The main problem with destructible environments is that it gives the player too much
freedom… this all breaks down when everything is destructible" — fixed by free planning
then a 60-second execution; multiplayer sends ordered deterministic destruction commands
and state-syncs rigid bodies; join-in-progress (replaying the log) was the hard part.
Create mod: speed/stress budgets, in-world mechanisms over GUIs. Vintage Story: budgeted
mechanical power. Eco: player-written, game-enforced laws in place of protection.
https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/combining-bombastic-heists-with-a-fully-destructible-voxel-world-in-i-teardown-i- ·
https://80.lv/articles/teardown-developer-breaks-down-multiplayer-and-voxel-destruction-tech ·
https://www.engadget.com/2017-03-12-breath-of-the-wild-gdc-talk.html ·
https://wiki.vintagestory.at/Mechanical_power

**Knowledge-gated adventure.** Animal Well (Billy Basso): four designed layers, the last
"not exactly intended to be easily solvable by one person".
https://thinkygames.com/features/interview-how-animal-well-is-using-secrets-and-mysteries-to-be-a-different-kind-of-metroidvania/

## D. Design theory

**Koster.** Content creation: rate × developers must exceed consumption and never does;
~54 dev-hours per 2 h MUD quest (1:27), ~1:460 commercially; exits are generation,
dynamic environments, player-created content, other players. 2025: "If a problem
basically has one answer, we often call it a puzzle… You can stack a bunch of puzzles…
and build a game out of them." Treadmills are "a very bad place to be".
https://www.raphkoster.com/games/snippets/content-creation/ ·
https://www.raphkoster.com/2025/11/03/game-design-is-simple-actually/ ·
https://www.raphkoster.com/2007/04/23/the-game-without-treadmills/

**Raids as coordination puzzles.** Luke Smith on Vault of Glass: "cooperation required",
"voice required", mechanics unexplained, no matchmaking; after mastery "your
conversations… evolve from strategy talk to talking about your day". FFXIV: "a giant
jump rope". WoW's Hazzikostas on addons: "We're not looking to turn all our raid
encounters into giant puzzles for people to solve" [S — article body truncated].
https://kotaku.com/q-a-the-lead-designer-of-destinys-vault-of-glass-1647227058 ·
https://blog.playstation.com/2018/11/13/square-enix-qa-how-omega-became-a-boss-in-final-fantasy-xiv/

**Dan Cook (Lost Garden).** Friendship via proximity, similarity, reciprocity,
disclosure; Dunbar layers — "a guild system that allows for 100,000 members is
functionally worse… than a guild capped at 150"; non-zero-sum first; grief-test your
systems. Kind Games (GDC 2024): rival resources breed toxicity, information is non-rival;
"soft co-op"; "if you can starve griefers of victims, griefing falls off"; ~5% always
defect; toxicity in a first session → 32× churn (League of Legends).
https://lostgarden.com/2017/01/27/game-design-patterns-for-building-friendships/ ·
https://lostgarden.com/2023/07/08/kind-games-designing-for-prosocial-multiplayer/

**Quarterbacking.** Fixes: hidden/private information, real-time pressure, limited
communication, execution load.
https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/35063/board-games/thought-of-the-day-quarterbacking-in-co-op-games ·
https://www.meeplemountain.com/articles/benching-the-quarterback-how-to-deal-with-alpha-players-in-co-op-games/

**Procedural puzzle generation.** De Kegel & Haahr survey (IEEE ToG 2020): 32 methods,
11 categories; solvability is tractable for state-space puzzles (Sokoban via reverse
search, CSPs, sliding puzzles, mazes); insight, narrative, multi-mechanic puzzles and
difficulty estimation remain open. LLM-era: Todd et al. 2023 (Sokoban levels),
ScriptDoctor 2025 (LLM writes PuzzleScript, BFS playtester feeds back), Enigmata
(generator/verifier pairs). Consensus: generators produce instances of known mechanics,
not new ideas.
https://www.scss.tcd.ie/mads.haahr/papers/de-kegel-2020-transactions.pdf ·
https://ianparberry.com/research/sokoban/ ·
https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05817 · https://arxiv.org/html/2506.06524v1
