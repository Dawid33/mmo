FIXED 2026-09-20 (working tree on develop, user had not asked for a commit). The "jitter and lag crossing chunk borders" after [[creative-movement-landed]] was pure **simulation**: reproducible headless with one player on a perfectly flat floor (20–67 of 85 walking ticks anomalous: sink, free-fall through the skin, pop up with `grounded=false`, speed hitch). Not client rendering, not netcode, not mispredicts. It was everywhere, just most visible where voxel borders coincide with collider borders.

Root causes, all in the vendored forks (upstream bugs), all around "a cast that starts exactly one skin (`target_distance`) from a surface — where the KCC parks the capsule every tick":
1. `parry …/shape_cast_support_map_support_map.rs`: target distance is a ray cast against the shape rounded by the skin; starting ON that surface it MISSES or returns the raw GJK search direction as normal (7° off a flat face). Fix: classify starts within skin×1.01 by true closest-points distance; refine Converged normals from closest points at the impact pose; closing-speed test tolerant to normal noise.
2. `parry …/shape_cast_voxels_shape.rs`: search seeded from the UN-loosened AABB (floor not in it when hovering a skin above) and the search box SHIFTED instead of growing → voxel under the capsule never tested when the AABB's leading face is near a voxel border (periodic free-fall tick). Fix: loosen by target distance, grow leading edge, retire only rows the swept AABB left.
3. Same file: every voxel cast as a free-standing cuboid → internal-edge (ghost) normals on flat floors. Fix: `without_internal_edges` zeroes normal components pointing through a covered face — covered per `VoxelState::free_faces()` OR per an `InternalEdgeProbe` (world point query a skin across the face at the contact). Applied BEFORE the separating-start filter and min-TOI pick. The probe is what handles chunk↔chunk, block-scale↔chisel-scale and collapsed-cuboid↔voxels seams — read-only, no voxel-state stitching, no rollback/hash impact (this resolves the seam item deferred in [[multi-scale-colliders-landed]]).
4. `rapier …/character_controller.rs`: all 6 KCC casts go through new `QueryPipeline::cast_shape_on_continuous_surfaces`; `snap_to_ground` now gated on the DESIRED translation heading down (upstream gated on the result having a downward residue, which only sloppy normals provided) and also runs after a climb.

FOLLOW-UP same day (uncommitted at time of writing): commit 4173c4e made the KCC 3-5x more expensive in production terms (extra GJK per voxel, snap cast every tick) and tipped the client into the replay spiral; fixed by restructuring `cast_shapes_voxels_shape_with_probe`: (1) resting-contact pass first — support-function `separation_bound` then `dispatcher.contact`, first voxel within the skin returns toi=0 with no ray cast (same voxel order => same winner); (2) `faces_motion` skips voxels whose faces looking into the motion are all covered; (3) per-voxel ray cast capped at best TOI, propagation stops past it; KCC snaps only when `!result.grounded`. move_shape on generated chiselled terrain 894/1840 us -> 70/86 us (idle/walk, release); production-mode tick with player 105 us idle / 261 us walking. See [[sim-cost-measure-hash-off]].

Measured: flat floors → level to 0.002 units, constant speed, always grounded; generated terrain 360-tick walk: ungrounded 43→0; release tick cost unchanged on block-scale, −35% mean / −57% p90 on chisel-scale. Tests: `crates/game/tests/walking_smoothness.rs`, `crates/game/tests/shape_cast_grazing.rs` (both verified red on HEAD).

**Why:** user asked to find and fix the chunk-border jitter. **How to apply:** don't re-investigate. Known residuals: (a) walking UP 1-voxel terraces still dips speed for a tick (~20/360 ticks, worst 2.36 of 3.45 u/tick) — the capsule's round foot meets a 1-unit step at 32°, under the 45° ramp limit, so it is slope-slid not autostepped; lowering `max_slope_climb_angle` was TRIED and REJECTED (autostep's landing-slope check then refuses terraced landings → full stall ticks); (b) `cargo test -p rapier3d --lib` does not compile at HEAD (`ordermap` `drain()` API in physics_pipeline.rs) so the fork's own KCC tests can't run; (c) no live GUI feel pass done; client-side: camera smoothing in `interpolate.rs` is a per-frame 0.3 lerp (frame-rate dependent) and `cull_chunks`' change gate is defeated by frustum plane distances while moving — neither chunk-border specific, neither touched.
